# Cashier register improvements

1. **Park and continue.** Put the open ticket on hold with a staff-entered label or the first product as a fallback, then open a fresh sale. [Parking form and action](../public/register.js#L643)
2. **Per-register parking storage.** Parked tickets survive a page reload or browser restart on the same browser profile and stay scoped to the selected register. [Terminal storage](../public/register-workflow.js#L26)
3. **Visible parked count.** The ticket toolbar exposes the current count and responds to parking changes made from another tab in the same browser. [Count and screen-reader label](../public/register.js#L637)
4. **Version-pinned recall.** Reopening a hold verifies the original server session, terminal and menu version, then restores its lines, fulfillment and adjustments. [Recall validation](../public/register.js#L696)
5. **Active-ticket protection.** Recall remains disabled while another ticket has items. Staff can park or finish the current ticket before recalling a hold. [Parked-ticket controls](../public/register.js#L682)
6. **Confirmed discard.** Staff can remove a named hold through an explicit confirmation without changing the open ticket. [Discard flow](../public/register.js#L731)
7. **Register sales history.** Recent sales show the number, amount, timestamp, tender, menu version and refund status for this register. [Sales history](../public/register.js#L1012)
8. **Historical receipt and reprint.** Open and print a recent receipt while keeping the current register ticket in place. [Historical receipt renderer](../public/register.js#L602)
9. **Staff note.** Add a 240-character internal note with a live character count and preserve it through refresh, parking and sale submission. [Sale adjustments](../public/register.js#L1031)
10. **Discount presets.** Apply or remove a 10% staff discount or 15% service recovery discount against the server quote. [Discount choices](../public/register-workflow.js#L4)
11. **Tip quick amounts.** Set no tip, $1, $2 or $5 in the adjustment dialog. [Tip buttons](../public/register.js#L1039)
12. **Custom tip.** Enter a tip of up to $50 in whole cents with inline validation before applying it. [Tip validation](../public/register.js#L1046)
13. **Itemized adjustment totals.** Display authoritative discount, tax, tip and grand total in the ticket, adjusted payment review and printed receipt. [Ticket totals](../public/register.js#L371), [Payment and receipt breakdown](../public/register.js#L594)
14. **Live sold-out controls.** Store-specific inventory updates disable unavailable catalogue and quick keys without a reload. [Inventory refresh](../public/register.js#L331)
15. **Remaining quantities.** Limited-stock keys show the units left after accounting for this ticket. Quantity controls prevent adding past that limit. [Quantity calculation](../public/register.js#L311)
16. **Recoverable stock rejection.** If stock changes before payment, the register refreshes availability, keeps the ticket and returns staff to editing. [Payment error handling](../public/register.js#L555)
17. **Editable quick keys.** Assign four distinct products to terminal-specific shortcuts that persist in the browser. [Quick key editor](../public/register.js#L1059)
18. **Cashier keyboard guide.** A visible shortcuts panel explains keyboard navigation, including F6 for parking, F7 for recall, F8 for recent sales and F9 for adjustments. [Shortcut guide](../public/register.js#L1088)
19. **One-action search reset.** The reset button or Escape clears the query and category together and returns focus to search. [Search reset](../public/register.js#L1099)
20. **Keyboard product navigation.** Arrow keys move between category buttons and within the product grid, skipping unavailable items. Arrow Down from search focuses the first available product. [Register keyboard navigation](../public/register.js#L777)
21. **Fixed desktop payment area.** The product grid and ticket lines scroll independently at workstation sizes so payment remains in view. [Desktop layout](../public/register.css#L307)
22. **Larger touch targets.** Category, service, parking, quantity and cash controls meet a 44px target height, with 44×44px quantity buttons. [Touch sizing](../public/register.css#L282)
23. **Add cash bills.** Add $1, $5, $10 or $20 to the received amount and calculate change as bills are counted. [Cash denomination buttons](../public/register.js#L543)
24. **Last-used tender.** Start the next sale with the last successfully recorded payment method for that register in the same tab. [Tender selection and persistence](../public/register.js#L492)
25. **Live customer counter.** Open the display for this register and broadcast ticket, payment, completion and idle states with no staff notes or transaction IDs. [Counter link](../public/register.html#L32), [Snapshot fields](../public/register.js#L347), [Display channel](../public/register-workflow.js#L42)

An isolated Edge run exercised the register at 1280×800, 1366×768, 1920×1080 and 320px wide. It checked a held ticket recalled on Menu v1 after a v2 rollout, adjustments and cash change, recent receipt retrieval, inventory limits, quick key persistence and counter message privacy. Parked tickets and quick key assignments are stored in the register’s browser profile.
